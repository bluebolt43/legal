ID Photo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

ID Photo respects your privacy.

Photo Processing

Photos selected or captured by the user are processed locally on the user's device. The developer does not upload, collect, store, or share these photos.

Saved and Shared Photos

Processed photos are saved or shared only when the user chooses to perform those actions. The destination and handling of shared photos are controlled by the selected application or service.

Account Information

ID Photo does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Optional Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to support the developer. Payments are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store complete payment card information. This purchase does not unlock or restrict any app functionality.

Bug Reports

Users may voluntarily send feedback or bug reports through an email application. Information is transmitted only after the user reviews and sends the email. The content of the email is handled according to the user's email provider.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal information through ID Photo.

Children's Privacy

ID Photo is not specifically directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
